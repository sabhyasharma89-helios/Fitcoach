FitOS privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

FitOS is a personal, single-user app. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no advertising.

What FitOS stores

Everything you enter (workouts, food, weight, waist, steps, settings) is stored only on your device, in the app's local database. Nothing is uploaded anywhere by FitOS. You can export a backup file yourself from Settings; where that file goes is your choice.

Health Connect data (Android app)

If you allow it, the Android app reads the following from Health Connect: steps, weight, exercise sessions, active calories burned and (optionally) heart rate. FitOS only reads this data; it never writes to Health Connect.

The data is used only to show your daily steps, your weight trend and your completed exercise sessions inside FitOS, on your device. It is not sold, shared, transferred, or used for advertising, credit or insurance decisions, and it is not used to train any model.

You can withdraw access at any time in Android Settings › Security and privacy › Privacy › Health Connect › App permissions › FitOS, or from the Settings screen in FitOS.

Network requests

When you scan a barcode, FitOS sends only the barcode number to Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org) to look up the product's nutrition values. No personal data is sent.

Deleting data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all FitOS data from the device. Settings › Delete all data does the same from inside the app.
